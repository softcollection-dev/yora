Delete your account in the app

1. Open the Yora app and log in. 2. Menu ☰ › Settings › Delete my account. 3. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

What is deleted

• Staff account: your login and mobile number are deleted. Your name stays on bills and records you made, because the Food Place must keep its business records for accounts and taxes. • Owner account: the whole Food Place is deleted - all staff logins, menu, tables, orders, bills, reports, expenses, stock and settings. This cannot be undone. Download your reports (Excel) first.
