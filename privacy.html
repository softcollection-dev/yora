Who we are

Yora : Restaurant Hotel Billing ("Yora") is billing software for Food Places (restaurants, hotels, dhabas, cafés and other food businesses). Yora is made and run by soft collection, a sole proprietorship of Manisha Sharad Adsule, Rama Udyan, Plot No 60, Phase No 3, Pandharpur Road, Miraj 416410, Maharashtra. ("we", "us"). Yora is used only by the owner and staff of a Food Place. There are no customer accounts, no online ordering and no delivery service in Yora.

Data stored in Yora

• Food Place details entered by the owner: name, address, phone, GSTIN, FSSAI number, UPI ID, bill settings, taxes, charges and offers. • Owner and staff: name, mobile number and role. The 6-digit PIN is stored only in hashed (scrambled) form; we cannot see it. • Business records: menu, tables, orders, kitchen tickets (KOT), bills, payment mode and amount recorded, discounts and their reasons, expenses, stock, day closing and an activity log of staff actions. • A customer's name and mobile number, only if staff type them on a bill.

What we do NOT collect

• We do not take, hold or process any money. Customers pay the Food Place directly by cash, UPI or card; Yora only records it. We never ask for card numbers, UPI PIN, OTP or bank passwords. • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. • No advertising, no ad tracking and we do not sell or rent any data.

Why the data is used

Only to run Yora for the Food Place: log in staff, take orders, print KOTs and bills, make reports and Excel files, keep the activity log, protect accounts from misuse, give support, and follow the law.

Who is responsible for which data

• The Food Place owner decides what business and customer data to enter and why. For that data the Food Place is responsible, and we store and process it only on the Food Place's behalf. • For owner and staff login details we are responsible.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored on Supabase cloud servers (region: Mumbai, India (ap-south-1)). Our cloud provider may keep normal server logs (such as time of access) for security. All data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Database rules make sure each Food Place can see only its own data, and staff only what their role allows.

Data on the phone

• The login session, language, theme colour and printer choice are kept on the phone. • For offline billing, the menu, tables and bill settings are saved on the phone, and bills made without internet stay on that phone until they upload. Do not clear the app data or uninstall the app while bills are waiting to upload. • Fingerprint login is checked by Android itself. Your fingerprint never leaves the phone and we never receive it.

Phone permissions

• Internet: to save and sync data. • Fingerprint (optional): quick login. • Bluetooth / Nearby devices (optional): to print on a Bluetooth printer.

Sharing

We share data only with our cloud provider (Supabase) to run the service, or when the law requires it. Data leaves Yora when you choose to share it: printing, sharing a PDF / Excel file or sending a bill on WhatsApp.

How long data is kept

• While the account is active, so bills and reports stay available for accounts and taxes. • Owner deletes the account: the Food Place and all its data are deleted from the database at once. Copies in the cloud provider's automatic backups are removed in its normal backup cycle. • Staff deletes the account: the login and mobile number are deleted. The staff name stays on bills and records made by that person, because the Food Place must keep its business records.

Your rights

• See and download your data: Reports › PDF / Excel. • Correct data: edit it in the app, or ask the owner. • Delete data: Settings › Delete my account, or email us. • Complain: write to the Grievance Officer below.

Security

PINs are hashed, connections are encrypted, and access is limited by role. No system is 100% secure; keep your PIN secret and switch off staff who leave. If a data breach happens we will act quickly and inform the people affected and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Yora is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be shown in the app and on our website.
